# Privacy Policy — IITC MapKit

Effective date: 2026-11-12

IITC MapKit is an independent native client for the Ingress Intel map. It is not
an official Niantic or IITC release and is not affiliated with Niantic. This
policy explains what the app does with your data.

## No account required

The app works without signing in. Built-in sample maps use fictional data and
require no account and no network access.

## Optional Niantic / Ingress sign-in

Signing in is optional and enables live Intel data. Apple sign-in uses the
official Ingress sign-in page inside the app. Google sign-in continues in Safari.
When you start that flow, the Safari extension transfers the Intel session for
`intel.ingress.com` to the app on the same device. It does not have access to the
Google sign-in page or your Google cookies. The verified Intel session is kept
locally so you stay signed in. **Disconnect** stops Intel access and automatic
reconnection.

## Data stays on your device

Map cache, drawings, inventory, COMM extracts, player tracking and redeem
history are stored locally on your device. The developer operates no backend
server and does not upload, collect or receive this data. Deleting the app
removes it.

## No tracking, no ads, no analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs.
It does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not sell or share
personal data with third parties.

## Network access

When signed in, the app talks only to Niantic's Ingress Intel endpoints to load
map and COMM data. Map rendering uses Apple MapKit, which requests map tiles
from Apple under Apple's own privacy terms. No other network services are used.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone.
